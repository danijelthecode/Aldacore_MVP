How Newcode works

Modern legal work is complex. The tools supporting it don’t have to be.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Connect every system your firm already relies on to one backbone, and run work end to end, from Outlook to iManage, Teams to OneDrive, and all the platforms that power your daily work.

    Shown in Cowork: one instruction that reaches Outlook and OneDrive, and its run.

  2. 02

    Contextualize

    Retrieve information and knowledge intelligently from your connected systems. Our retrieval agent plans, searches, evaluates, and iterates until the context is right, guided by firm-defined taxonomy, labels, and ethical walls.

    Shown in Nova: court dates extracted from a scheduling order, next to the calendar entry they feed.

  3. 03

    Create

    Create case assessments, conduct discovery, perform analysis and research, draft documents, prepare trial submissions, provide strategic advice, conduct due diligence, support negotiations, and manage deals, all from your contextualized data.

    Shown: a mutual non-disclosure agreement in redline, beside the documents it sits with.

  4. 04

    Control

    Gain full oversight of how knowledge, data, and AI behave at every level, with governed, auditable, and matter-level configuration, granular permissions, cloud or local deployment options, and the flexibility to use the LLM and interface that work best for your firm.

    Shown in Aurora: guardrails, agent policies and a human review step.

Prototype notes

Not part of the homepage. Every placeholder and open point labeled above, in page order. Select one to jump to it.

    • Product UI: the interface cards are HTML rebuilds of product UI shown on newcode.ai, with the same demo content. Three documents inside them (the Project Meridian request list, the calendar entry and the non-disclosure agreement) are real images from the site. Swap in 2x exports from the Nova Figma file before production.
    • Plates: the wall, desk, stone, paper and room photographs behind the UI are generated plates made with the Newcode image plates method, with no people, text or screens in them. Replace with commissioned photography if preferred.
    • Photos: real photos of offices, customers and the team are still needed. The page uses the photos already on newcode.ai, plus a still from the Ræder Bing film.
    • Typeface: the page is set in Inter, the brief’s fallback. Grain is licensed and its font file is not bundled.
    • Links: product, academy, news and contact links open the live newcode.ai pages in a new tab. In production they stay on the site.
