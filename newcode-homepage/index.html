How Newcode works

Newcode connects knowledge, systems and AI into one intelligent environment designed around the way lawyers actually work.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Connect every system your firm already relies on to one backbone, and run work end to end, from Outlook to iManage, Teams to OneDrive, and all the platforms that power your daily work.

    Shown in Cowork: one instruction that reaches Outlook and OneDrive.

  2. 02

    Contextualize

    Retrieve information and knowledge intelligently from your connected systems. Our retrieval agent plans, searches, evaluates, and iterates until the context is right, guided by firm-defined taxonomy, labels, and ethical walls.

    Shown in Nova: court dates extracted from a scheduling order.

  3. 03

    Create

    Create case assessments, conduct discovery, perform analysis and research, draft documents, prepare trial submissions, provide strategic advice, conduct due diligence, support negotiations, and manage deals, all from your contextualized data.

    Shown in the Word Add In: a loan agreement populated from a term sheet.

  4. 04

    Control

    Gain full oversight of how knowledge, data, and AI behave at every level, with governed, auditable, and matter-level configuration, granular permissions, cloud or local deployment options, and the flexibility to use the LLM and interface that work best for your firm.

    Shown in Aurora: guardrails, agent policies and a human review step.
