How Newcode works

The configurable AI harness for the legal industry

Connect Contextualize Create Control

Modern legal work is complex. The tools supporting it don’t have to be.

Connect

Connect every system your firm already relies on to one backbone, and run work end to end, from Outlook to iManage, Teams to OneDrive.

Shown in Cowork: one instruction that reaches Outlook and OneDrive, and its run.

Contextualize

Our retrieval agent plans, searches, evaluates, and iterates until the context is right, guided by firm-defined taxonomy, labels, and ethical walls.

Shown in Nova: court dates extracted from a scheduling order.

Create

Create case assessments, conduct discovery, perform analysis and research, draft documents, and manage deals, all from your contextualized data.

Shown: NDAs arriving through the legal front door and filing into a single intake list.

Control

Gain full oversight of how knowledge, data, and AI behave at every level, with governed, auditable, and matter-level configuration and granular permissions.

Shown in Aurora: guardrails, agent policies and a human review step.
